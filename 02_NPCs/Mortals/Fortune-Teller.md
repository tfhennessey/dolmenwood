# Fortune-Teller

## At a Glance

Minor oracles and seers—some genuine, some deluded, and some charlatans. Wander from settlement to settlement, consulting the fates in return for a small consideration.

**Kindred:** Usually humans or breggles.

## Special Rules

**Telling fortunes:** A fortune-teller may be consulted about a character’s plan or quest. The fee is 1d10gp, traditionally paid in silver. The result hints at whether the proposed course of action will end in weal or woe.
