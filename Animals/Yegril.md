# Yegril

## At a Glance

Gigantic, fluffy moose with luxuriant, purple fur, moon-yellow eyes, and sensitive, five-toed paws. Their fuzzy, orange antlers are long and droopy, only becoming erect during the mating season. Yegrils are gentle creatures that mewl plaintively as they rise up on their hind legs to strip moss from high branches. Flee if attacked.
