# Cockatrice

![[Cockatrice.png]]

## At a Glance

4′ long cockerel-like creatures with wattled necks, scaled tails, and dragon-like bodies. Feared for their ability to petrify any creature they touch.

## Special Rules

**Petrification:** The victim must Save Versus Hold or be permanently turned to stone.

**Rock eating:** Cockatrices drag petrified victims to their lair for consumption. They smash the statues by toppling them and gobble down the gravel by the gullet-full.
