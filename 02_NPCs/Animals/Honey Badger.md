# Honey Badger

## At a Glance

Squat, white-furred badgers that are slick with a honeylike substance oozing from their pores. Honey badgers are nocturnal omnivores known for their curiosity and ravenous hunger. Their flesh is grossly fatty, but their slime is delectable.
