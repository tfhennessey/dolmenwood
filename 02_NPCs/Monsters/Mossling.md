# Mossling

## At a Glance

4′ tall, gnarled, woody humanoids with mossy hair and beards. Live in isolated communities in gloomy, dank, forest dells. Renowned as brewers and cheese-makers.

## Special Rules

**Resilience:** +4 bonus to Saving Throws against fungal spores or poisons. +2 bonus to all other Saving Throws.

**Weapons:** Mosslings favour clubs (1d4), hand axes (1d6), and slings (1d4).

**Armour:** Mosslings wear bark armour (DPB). Without armour, they have AC 10.

**Plant/fungus symbiosis:** Each mossling’s moist, earthy flesh hosts a number of symbiotic plants, moulds, or fungi. See Fertile Flesh.

**Fungi:** Mosslings carry a pouch of useful fungus foraged in the deep woods: 1d3 portions of Blood Canker (DPB), Hogscap (DPB), or Grinning Jenny (DCB).

**Main settlement:** Orbswallow (DCB).
