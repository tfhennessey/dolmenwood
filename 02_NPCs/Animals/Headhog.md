# Headhog

## At a Glance

Black-spined, flea-ridden hedgehogs with long, pink tongues that they use to catch insects. Creep out of the undergrowth to sleep on the heads of campers, infesting them with fleas, and licking their ear holes with their probing tongues. Flee when discovered.
