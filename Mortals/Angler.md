# Angler

## At a Glance

Fisherfolk bearing nets, rods, tackle, bait boxes, and buckets. If encountered on water, anglers are afloat on rafts or rowing boats.

## Special Rules

**Rations:** There is a 3-in-6 chance of anglers carrying 2d6 rations of fresh fish, which they may be willing to sell (1gp per ration). See Fishing in the Dolmenwood Campaign Book to determine the type of fish caught.
