# Merchant

## At a Glance

Organised traders who travel between settlements in well-armed convoys, buying and selling trade goods.

**Weapons:** Club (d4), dagger (d4), or staff (d4).
**Kindred:** Usually humans or breggles.

## Special Rules

**Wagons:** 1 wagon per merchant encountered.

**Wealth:** Roll 1d6 to determine the wealth carried by each wagon. The listed gp value of wealth may be in the form of coinage or valuable trade goods (e.g. minerals, silks, spices, furs, etc.).

**Guards:** The number of convoy guards is determined by the wealth roll and the number of wagons. Use fighter stats for guards (p105).
