## Special Rules

**Swoop:** Dive onto victims visible from above. If the victim is surprised, the attack inflicts double damage. On an Attack Roll of 18 or more, a Small or Medium victim can be carried away.

**Eggs:** 50% of nests contain 1d6 eggs or chicks.

**Trainable:** Can be trained, if captured when young.
