# Bat, Vampire

## At a Glance

Black-furred, red-eyed, bats with a 5′ wingspan, crooked fangs, and an insatiable appetite for blood. Bred by the Nag-Lord; roost in the twisted woods around its demesne.

## Special Rules

**Echolocation:** Navigate by sound, rather than vision. Unaffected by effects that impair, modify, or rely on sight. Blinded by magical silence.

**Unconsciousness:** The victim must Save Versus Hold or fall unconscious for 1d10 Rounds.

**Blood drain:** A vampire bat may drain blood from an unconscious victim: 1d4 automatic damage per Round. A victim killed by blood drain must Save Versus Doom or become a ghoul after 24 hours.
