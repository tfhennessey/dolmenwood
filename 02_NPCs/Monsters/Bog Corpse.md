# Bog Corpse

## At a Glance

Sodden corpses of hapless mortals who died, accursed, in bogs and swamps. Inhabited by the spirits of marsh-fires, they rise to wreak death and jealous vengeance upon the living.

## Special Rules

**Undead:** Silent before attacking. Immune to biological effects (e.g. disease, poison) and mind-affecting spells (e.g. Vapours of Dream, Paralysation, Dominate).

**Dark sight:** Can see normally without light.

**Throttle:** Upon a successful hit with a Damage Roll of 4 or greater, the bog corpse clasps its hands around the victim’s throat and begins to strangle them. The victim automatically suffers 1d6 damage per Round until the bog corpse is killed. A victim killed in this way is dragged into the bog and rises the following night as a bog corpse.

**In melee:** Characters in melee with a bog corpse must Save Versus Hold or suffer a –2 penalty to Attack Rolls, hypnotised by the flickering green light burning in the monster’s chest cavity.

**If killed:** The unholy green flame in the bog corpse’s chest explodes. All within 10′ suffer 1d6 damage (Save Versus Blast for half).
