# Fly, Giant

## At a Glance

3′ long bluebottles that subsist on carrion and offal. May also attack bloodied characters. Make an infuriating, droning buzzing when in flight.

**Leap:** After attacking, a giant fly can leap away from its victim—up to 30′. This is in addition to any movement the fly might have made in the movement initiative phase this Round.
