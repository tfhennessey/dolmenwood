# Griffon

## At a Glance

Large, rapacious predators with the body of a lion and the head, wings, and claws of an eagle. Prey on horses and those who ride them.

## Special Rules

**Taming:** If reared from hatchlings, griffons can be trained as fiercely loyal mounts. Their instinct to attack horses cannot be trained out, however: only if they pass a Morale Check can they be commanded to not attack.
