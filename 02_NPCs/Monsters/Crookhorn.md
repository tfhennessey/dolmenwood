# Crookhorn

## At a Glance

7′ tall, feral, disease-ridden breggles, twisted by the evil magic of their master, Atanuwë. Roam northern Dolmenwood as pillagers, brigands, and burners of villages.

## Special Rules

**Weapons:** Crookhorns favour clubs (1d4) and spears (1d6).

**Armour:** Crookhorns wear a rough patchwork of spiked leather and chainmail. Without armour, they have AC 11.

**Disease:** Anyone who comes into close contact with a crookhorn (including being bitten or butted by one) must Save Versus Doom or be afflicted by a nasty infection (see Crookhorn Diseases). All can be cured with Lankswith (DPB).

**Marauders:** Crookhorns delight in the capture, torture, and (inevitable) roasting of other sentients.
