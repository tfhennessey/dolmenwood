# Fomorian

![[z_attachments/monster images/Fomorian.png]]

## At a Glance

Giant (12′–14′ tall) humanoids, with pale, translucent skin, lumpy flesh, ungainly limbs, lank hair, and a single huge eye. Hoard gems and lost secrets in deep caverns and desolate regions.

## Special Rules

**Dark sight:** Can see normally without light.

**Gaze:** A fomorian may gaze upon a target within 60′, who must Save Versus Hold or be blinded for 2d4 Rounds. The fomorian also knows the victim’s aims and intents.

**Mutations:** Due to exposure to the weird magic of the underworld, each fomorian possesses a mutation—roll on the Mutations table.

**Herders:** Fomorians raise subterranean animals for food, typically cave salamanders (p113), giant earthworms (p113), or ooze salamanders (p116).
