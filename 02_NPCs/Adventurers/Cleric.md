# Cleric

## At a Glance

Ne’er-do-wells and righteous questers that one might encounter in the wilds. Example stat blocks for characters of all nine Classes, at Levels 1, 3, and 5. These may be convenient for use when PCs encounter other adventurers, or for use as bandits, guild members, town guards, etc.

## Level 1

**Gear:** Chainmail + shield. Longsword (1d8). Shortbow + 20 arrows (1d6).

## Level 3

**Gear:** Plate mail + shield. Longsword (1d8). Shortbow + 20 arrows (1d6). Vial of holy water.

**Spells:** Lesser Healing, Mantle of Protection.

**Companions:** 1d6 acolytes.

## Level 5

**Gear:** Plate mail + Holy Shield. Holy Longsword (1d8+2, +2 Attack). Shortbow + 20 arrows (1d6). 3 vials of holy water. Prismatic Elixir. Scroll of Cure Affliction.

**Spells:** Lesser Healing, Light, Bless, Hold Person.

**Companions:** 1d4 wardens, 2d6 acolytes.

## Special Rules

**Kindred:** Usually humans, occasionally other mortals.

**Holy order:** Clerics of Level 3 / 5 are members of a holy order.

`dice: [[Cleric^holyorder]]`

| dice: 1d6 | Holy Order |
| --------- | ---------- |
| 1–2 | St Faxis: +2 to saves against arcane magic; arcane spell-casters suffer –2 to saves against cleric’s spells. |
| 3–4 | St Sedge: Lay on hands once a day (1 HP / Level). |
| 5–6 | St Signis: +1 Attack vs undead; harms undead even without silver or magic weapons. |

^holyorder

## Possessions and Hoards

**Possessions:** In addition to the listed gear, adventurers typically carry 2d8gp per Level.

**Hoards:** Adventurers usually live in a settlement, so are not encountered in a lair in the wild. When using adventurer stat blocks for NPCs who do live in the wild (e.g. bandits), the Referee should decide on appropriate treasure types (see Placing Treasure, DCB). For example, a large, successful bandit gang might have a hoard of C7 + R11 + M9 in their hidden camp in the wild.
