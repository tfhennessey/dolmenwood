Recap:
After much heming and hawing you all decied to attack the crookhorns and see if anyone worth rescuing was still alive. With a combination of Reah sleepy smoke, Wobobolds firm grasp and some good old fashioned neck slitting you were able to take care of the 4 surviving crookhorns. It turns out they killed the caravan guards and moved the wagon to their camp. But they were attacked by an orge yesterday. The orge killed a large number of the crookhorns and stole the children, probably to eat. You freed the adult prisoners and sent the back to Prigwort with Destin, and you all followed the ogre's trail. You came to his cave and found the children locked in a cage. You were able to free the children without waking the sleeping ogre, but found he had the chest of coins from the caravan in his posession. What do you all want to do?
