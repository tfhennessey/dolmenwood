# False Unicorn

## At a Glance

White-furred deer with a single horn in the centre of the forehead. Females have a short, straight horn, and males a single, branched antler. From a distance these animals (especially the females) may be mistaken for unicorns. Gamy and reasonably tasty flesh. Timid and skittish.
