# Bog Salamander

![[z_attachments/monster images/Bog Salamander.png]]

## At a Glance

Translucent white, 8′ long, carnivorous amphibian, bulbous eyes that glow like lambent moons. Creebians with toothless maws, glassy claws, and creep through the undergrowth in search of prey.

## Special Rules

**Amphibious:** Can breathe air and water.

**Eye glow:** Anyone within 60′ seeing the glow of a bog salamander’s eyes must Save Versus Hold or be bedazzled: –2 penalty to Armour Class, –2 penalty to Attack Rolls against the bog salamander, Speed halved. This state lasts as long as the victim remains in the bog salamander’s presence.

**Slain victims:** Bodies are dragged to the bog salamander’s lair and slavered up in its toothless maw.
