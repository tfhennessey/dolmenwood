During the last session you were poised to take care of the Ogre and hopefully get away with it's treasure chest. However you found he was missing from it's cave. You tracked the giant brute though the woods to another [[Crookhorn]] encampment. A large one. You watched in disbelief as nearly 40 crookhorns took down the Ogre, roasted him, and ate him. You watched the camp throughout the night and gathered what information you could and reported it back to the guard captain in Prigwort. The number of Crookhorns combined with the siege equipment has him worried. He voluentold you all take a message to the Duke of Brakenwald. After the not too long journey and presenting your message you were escorted into the Duke's castle. You all stand in a plush waiting area. Your travel worn and battle damaged clothes at odds with the soft furniture and colorful tapestries. A handful of nobles are also in the large room with you. Several cover their noses and gracefully glide across to the other side of the room. A half dozen fully armored guards flank the walls. Their plate gleaming in the light of bright wall lamps. Their faces are covered with slitted visors and they hold spears with shortswords at their hips. Not long later a pompus looking man in small spectacles opens the door and quietly speaks someone's name as if he were in church. A pair of the nobles quickly take their leave and follow him. A few hours later you are the last people in the room. The man opens the door again, clears his throat and says with thinly veild distain "His Grace, the Duke of Brakenwold will see you now."
