# Drune—Braithmaid

![[z_attachments/monster images/Drune—Braithmaid.png]]

## At a Glance

Adolescent daughters of the Drune who wander the wilds, gathering herbs, roots, and fungi. Their youth belies their power, for they are versed in the arts of enchanted song.

## Special Rules

**Song:** A braithmaid may sing as she wanders the woods, weaving a subtle enchantment. Any who hear a Braithmaid’s song must Save Versus Hold or enter into a wondrous reverie, unable to meaningfully act until the Braithmaid either approaches or passes. These songs are not effective in combat.

**Talismans:** When wandering abroad, Braithmaids carry a magical ward to protect them from harm. See Talismans. Such talismans are only effective when worn by women and function only once before crumbling to dust. They are worth 500gp.

**Lair:** Typically live in a Drune family: a Cottager (p35), a Drunewife (p36), and 1d6–1 siblings.
