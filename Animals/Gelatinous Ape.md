# Gelatinous Ape

## At a Glance

Hairless, brightly coloured, transparent apes (3′ tall) that creep through the trees and undergrowth. Gelatinous apes are gentle-natured and show great kindness to lost travellers. Their jelly-like flesh is sweet and chewy.
